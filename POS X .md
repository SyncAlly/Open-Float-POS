OpenFloat POS X – Intelligent Commerce Platform Active
The OpenFloat POS X interface is designed as a modern, enterprise-grade business operating system rather than a simple sales screen. The entire platform follows a clean dashboard layout with responsive web and mobile interfaces, supporting desktops, tablets, POS touch terminals, and smartphones.

Upon login, users are directed to personalized dashboards depending on their roles such as Owner, Director, Branch Manager, Accountant, Storekeeper, Cashier, Human Resource Officer, Procurement Officer, Sales Representative, Delivery Rider, or System Administrator.
